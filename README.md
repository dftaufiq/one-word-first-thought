judge compares words two at a time on which is more interesting.

## The word

**psithurism** — the sound of wind whispering through the trees.

Picked from 12 candidates by internal pairwise judging against the published judge
brief (surprise, vividness, wit/sound/double meaning): rare enough to surprise,
vivid enough to hear, and the word itself whispers like the wind it names.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network,
no dependencies — just the word, exactly as the gate requires (UTF-8, 1–24 chars).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
# psithurism
```
